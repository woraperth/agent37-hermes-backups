# Thailand custom corporate gifts — evidence bank

Session research covered suppliers whose pages advertise logo-branded promotional items. Use this as a starting point, not as proof that every item meets a small MOQ.

## Supplier/category evidence

- **Premiums** — https://www.premiums.co.th/ — catalogue text lists pens/pencils, notebooks, bags, bottles and drinkware, gift sets, umbrellas, flash drives, power banks, electronics, keychains, desk items and other promotional goods; page advertises logo printing/design.
- **Inkman Online** — https://www.inkmanonline.com/18262976/ — service text describes logo printing on bags, glasses, tumblers/bottles, umbrellas, hats, pillows, keychains, crystal items, mouse pads and other gifts; page includes a statement about free layout from 10 items, but the surrounding example may be product-specific (especially apparel), so do not generalise it to all products.
- **Gift Manufactory** — https://www.giftmanufactory.com/promotional-gifts — page data lists branded notebooks, laser-engraved metal pens, canvas/RPET bags, bottles, umbrellas, travel products, wellness products and gift sets. One product description explicitly showed a 300-piece minimum for an RPET bag, illustrating why MOQ must be checked per item.
- **Udesi** — https://udesi.co/ — catalogue/filter includes notebooks, pens, flash drives, stainless bottles, umbrellas, keychains, mugs, speakers, phone accessories, bags, gift sets, office products and more. Its quantity selector prominently presents larger runs (100+), so small-order suitability is not established from the homepage.
- **Max Products** — https://maxproducts.co.th/ — catalogue/metadata advertises logo-branded spunbond bags, bottles, mugs, pens, umbrellas, fans, hats, keychains, gift sets, lunch boxes and related promotional goods.

## Research rules

- Separate **available in catalogue** from **MOQ confirmed <=10**.
- Do not claim a supplier's "minimum 10" applies across all categories unless the page clearly says so.
- If no quantity is published, report `MOQ not stated—confirm with supplier`.
- Exclude T-shirts and other size-dependent apparel when sizing is unavailable.
- For an availability-only request, omit prices rather than filling gaps with estimates.
- Ask about exact quantity, artwork/setup fee, production time, delivery, and whether one logo/design can be used across mixed items.
