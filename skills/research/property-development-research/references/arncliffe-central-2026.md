# Arncliffe Central timeline evidence (retrieved August 2026)

Use this as a worked example of reconciling stale off-the-plan marketing dates with newer primary sources. It is evidence for method, not a permanent promise about future dates.

## Key evidence

- NSW Government ministerial release, 19 March 2026: more than 800 homes were under construction; 311 social, 291 affordable, and 204 private-market apartments were described. It states social homes were on track for completion in 2027 and the entire Arncliffe Central project was expected to be completed in 2028.
  URL: https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/arncliffe-central-boosted-to-75-social-and-affordable-homes
- Billbergia community-engagement portal: project status `Under Construction`; construction-update links were listed through March 2026.
  URL: https://www.billbergia.com.au/arncliffe/
- Evolve Housing release: repeats the 2027 social-housing and 2028 whole-project timeline.
  URL: https://www.evolvehousing.com.au/media-release/new-arncliffe-homes-to-deliver-long-term-housing-security-for-local-community/
- Older project/partner material said completion was expected in late 2025, while later 2026 reporting described the project as heading toward completion and newer government material moved the whole-precinct expectation to 2028.
  URLs:
  - https://www.haronrobson.com.au/news/arncliffe-central-by-billbergia-to-bring-city-living-in-suburbia/
  - https://www.billbergia.com.au/billbergia-approaches-launch-of-game-changing-2-2-billion-concord-central/

## Reusable lesson

Always state that the date refers to a milestone: private stage, social/affordable component, or whole precinct. A construction-completion estimate does not establish an individual apartment's contractual settlement date. For finance preparation, direct the user to their contract's settlement/sunset clauses and advise a broker/conveyancer review rather than presenting a date as guaranteed.
