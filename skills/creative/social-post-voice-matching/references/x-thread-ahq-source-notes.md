# Example: AHQ framework source notes

## Sources checked

- The linked public X thread by @woraperth: a Thai numbered summary with a short personal setup, practical explanations, English terms followed by Thai clarification, and a final recommendation/link.
- The official Thumbnail Masterclass landing page: publicly describes AHQ as a framework about psychological triggers and visual cues that encourage clicks on educational videos. It does not publish the full internal lesson or a reliable expansion of the acronym.
- A public Tintin Smith X result describes a related three-part thumbnail evaluation around attention, clarity, and curiosity; this is useful context but should not be presented as the official AHQ acronym expansion without stronger primary-source support.

## Voice observations

- Conversational Thai, first person, and a reason for sharing the idea.
- Numbered sections, usually one practical point per section.
- Short paragraphs designed for mobile reading.
- English marketing/design terms are retained and explained in Thai.
- Uses concrete questions and examples rather than abstract definitions.
- Ends with a simple synthesis or a question the reader can reuse.

## Accuracy lesson

When drafting about AHQ, describe the verified high-level purpose: making educational thumbnails attract attention, communicate quickly, and create enough curiosity to earn a click. Do not state that A/H/Q officially mean a particular set of words unless the primary course material confirms it. Avoid implying access to paid course lessons based only on the public landing page.
